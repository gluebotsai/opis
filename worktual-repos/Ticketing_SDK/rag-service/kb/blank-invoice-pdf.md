# October invoice PDF is blank

## Issue
Subject: October invoice PDF is blank

Requester: Omar Farouk <omar@brightledger.test>

Bright Ledger downloaded the October invoice and every page is blank. The on-screen invoice still shows the line items.

## Reply
The October PDF used an empty export template. The on-screen invoice is the right one. Open it, choose Export PDF again, and download the new file. The line items will be on the pages.
