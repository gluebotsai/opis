# Calendar invites show the wrong time zone

## Issue
Subject: Calendar invites show the wrong time zone

Requester: Hannah Brooks <hannah@lumenfield.test>

Hannah is in London. Meetings she accepts from the New York office land two hours early on her phone, and the desktop calendar still shows the correct time. It started after she updated the mail app yesterday.

## Reply
The phone mail app picked up Eastern Time after yesterday's update. On the phone, set the mail app time zone to Europe/London and turn off automatic time zone. Delete the New York meetings and accept the invites again. Leave the desktop calendar as it is.
