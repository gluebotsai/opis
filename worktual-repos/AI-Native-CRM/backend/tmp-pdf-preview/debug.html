Introduction

Yaswanth, a technology and software company based in India with over 500 employees, is pleased to present this proposal to Hexaware. Hexaware is a leading IT services provider with a workforce of more than 2,000 professionals and a strong focus on modernising customer engagement, boosting agent productivity, and leveraging data‑driven insights.

Our AI‑for‑Enterprise offering – an AI Contact Centre combined with an AI Chat Bot – is purpose‑built for large technology organisations. It aligns directly with Hexaware’s strategic priorities and delivers measurable improvements across the customer service lifecycle.

Key capabilities that address Hexaware’s needs include:

  • Shift Scheduling: The system analyses historic call‑volume patterns and automatically optimises agent rosters, ensuring coverage during peak periods while reducing idle time.
  • Conversational Interface: Agents and customers interact with the AI without switching applications. The AI can retrieve customer records, ticket history, invoices, and CRM data instantly, and execute actions such as ticket creation, appointment scheduling, or follow‑up emails.
  • Automated Reporting & Analytics: Real‑time dashboards and on‑demand visualisations (e.g., “show today’s inbound volume across all channels”) are generated instantly, giving managers transparent insight into SLA compliance, resolution times, and agent performance.
  • Similar Ticket Detection & Knowledge‑Base Integration: The AI continuously scans incoming tickets, links related issues, and surfaces relevant SOPs, policies, and product documents, accelerating resolution and reducing duplicate effort.
  • Live Calendar Booking: The bot accesses live calendars, proposes open slots, and confirms appointments on the spot, eliminating the need for callers to navigate external sites.
  • Seamless Human Handover: When a caller is frustrated or requests a specialist, the AI places the caller on hold, routes them to an available agent, and supplies a complete summary of the conversation for a smooth transition.

By deploying this suite, Hexaware can expect higher agent efficiency, faster customer resolution, lower average handling time, and an enhanced overall customer experience – all supported by real‑time, data‑driven decision making.

We look forward to partnering with Hexaware to realise these outcomes.
